What is mapped here?

Road connectivity and building footprints come from OpenStreetMap. Marina / JLT loads first; the Marina–Dubai Mall corridor is bundled and other Dubai corridors can load on demand. Lane positions are reconstructed; untagged building heights are estimated. Arrival is at a mapped drivable road access near the building or resolved entrance. It may still require a driveway or walk.

Online search sends the entered place to OpenStreetMap Nominatim; Makani numbers go to Dubai Municipality. New corridors use public OSRM and Overpass services. These prototype services can be busy or unavailable.

 KNOW YOUR COVERAGE

Built from the map.

Every rendered branch follows a mapped way. Routes use shared OSM nodes, one-way directions, access tags and supported node-via turn restrictions. A geometric crossing alone never connects roads.

Lane geometry, estimated heights and building road-access points require further validation. This version cannot route to buildings outside its loaded area.

Makani entrance lookups are sourced from Dubai Municipality when you enter a Makani number. The number is sent to that official service for lookup. Official entrance records are kept separate from the OSM map; this app does not bundle a complete Makani address database.
